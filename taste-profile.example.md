## Scoring guidance

- 90-100: book immediately, tell us the moment tickets go on sale
- 70-89: strong candidate, put it in the weekly digest prominently
- 50-69: worth a mention if the week is quiet
- Below 50: do not surface

Weight an imminent on-sale date upward — a 65-scoring event going on sale on Friday is more useful to hear about than an 80-scoring event that has been bookable for months.
